About Child Protection Force

A Sri Lankan nonprofit founded in 2017 by attorney-at-law Milani Salpitikorala.

Our story

We work with law enforcement, child protection officers, educators and communities to help survivors access immediate care and long-term justice.

Mission

Protect and empower survivors through trauma-informed care, justice and reintegration.

Vision

A Sri Lanka where every child is safe, survivors are heard and justice is swift and compassionate.

Values

The best interests of children, education, survivor-centered practice, collaboration and accountability. The organization draws on the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child and Northern Ireland Principles.
